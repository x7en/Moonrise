# Moonrise

Top-down pixel survival game built in Godot 4.7.

Gather planks and scrap by day, patch up the fence rings around your farmhouse, set traps and upgrades, and try to hold out through the Blood Moon.

## Opening in Godot
1. Open Godot 4.7+ and click **Import**.
2. Pick `RedMoon/project.godot` and hit **F5** to play.

*(If you pulled over an older checkout, delete any local `RedMoon/.godot/` folder first so Godot re-imports the renamed scripts and sprites cleanly.)*

## Controls
- **WASD / Arrows** - Move
- **Space** - Spear swing (or clear zombies inside the house)
- **Left Click / F** - Shoot bow (Left Click also opens the upgrade menu on a nearby house/fence, or places a trap in trap mode)
- **R** - Reload bow
- **E** - Open/close house door & fence gates, enter/exit house, or hold **E** to search wrecks & tame stray dogs
- **1 / 2 / 3** - Toggle Spike, Bear Snare, or Decoy Lure placement (**Click / T** to place, **Right Click / Esc** to cancel)
- **Q** - Shockwave stun
- **U** - Open nearby house or fence upgrade menu
- **I** - Inventory
- **G** - Toggle tamed dog Follow / Guard
- **M** - Mute audio
- **F11** - Fullscreen
- **Esc** - Pause
